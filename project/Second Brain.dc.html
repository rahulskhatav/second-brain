Small models, long context

simonwillison.net/2025/small-models-long-context
Context windows grew faster than parameter counts, and the practical consequence is that a small model with a long window now beats a large one with a short one on most retrieval-shaped work. The piece walks through three deployments where a 7B model with 200k tokens of context outperformed a frontier model given only a summary, and argues the interesting frontier is no longer scale but what you choose to put in the window. It closes on cost: the long-context call is cheaper per useful answer, which is the number that ends arguments.
